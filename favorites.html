My Favorites

Out of all the recipes I enjoy making, there are always a few that stand out the most. These are some of my favorite sweet and savory dishes because of their flavors, memories, and the experience of making and sharing them with the people around me.

Sweet Favorites

Tiramisu

Tiramisu is another favorite because of the combination of espresso, mascarpone cream, and cocoa powder. I also like that it can be made with either traditional ladyfingers or cake layers.

New York Cheesecake

Guatemalan Chiles Rellenos are one of my favorite homemade dishes. I enjoy the combination of the seasoned meat and vegetables, fluffy egg coating, and tomato sauce.
